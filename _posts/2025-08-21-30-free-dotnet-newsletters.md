Keeping on top of .NET updates—and growing your engineering edge—doesn’t have to cost you money. Over the years, I've relied on high-quality newsletters to stay informed, inspired, and equipped. Here’s my list of 30 free and top-tier .NET newsletters you can start following today.

## Foundation .NET & C# Newsletters

1. [C# Digest](https://csharpdigest.netlify.app/ "C# Digest" rel="nofollow") (Weekly) — Hand-picked C# and .NET insights, with summaries that cut straight to the point.  
2. [.NET News](https://dotnetnews.co/ "DotNet News" rel="dofollow") (Daily) — Broad coverage from C#, Azure, ASP.NET, and more. Great for daily reading.  
3. [.NET Weekly](https://www.milanjovanovic.tech/newsletter "DotNet Weekly" rel="nofollow") (Weekly) — Real-world .NET guidance: clean architecture, EF tips, and more.  
4. [C# Insights](https://csharpdigest.netlify.app/insights "C# Insights" rel="nofollow") (Weekly) — Focused on language updates, performance tips, and tooling hacks.  
5. [ASP.NET Core News](https://www.aspnetweekly.net/ "ASP.NET Weekly" rel="nofollow") (Weekly) — Deep into web development: Blazor, APIs, SignalR, and more.  
6. [Awesome .NET Newsletter](https://awesome-dotnet.org/ "Awesome .NET Newsletter" rel="nofollow") (Weekly) — Highlights of libraries, frameworks, and NuGet packages to explore.


## Community & Ecosystem Focused

7. [dotNET Insight](https://visualstudiomagazine.com/newsletters/dotnet-insight.aspx "dotNET Insight" rel="nofollow") (Weekly) — Tooling and platform feature rollout news.  
8. [elmah.io Newsletter](https://blog.elmah.io/newsletter/ "elmah.io" rel="nofollow") (Weekly) — Logging, debugging, and practical .NET engineering stories.  
9. [.NET Foundation Newsletter](https://dotnetfoundation.org/newsletters "DotNet Foundation" rel="nofollow") (Monthly) — Updates on open-source projects and foundation news.  
10. [DotNetKicks](https://dotnetkicks.com/ "DotNetKicks" rel="nofollow") (Daily) — Community-voted .NET articles and resources.



## Developer Career & Best Practices

11. [Dev Secrets](https://www.devsecrets.net/ "Dev Secrets" rel="nofollow") (Weekly) — Sharable, insightful advice on .NET architecture, Azure, and developer best practices.  
12. [Programming Digest](https://programmingdigest.net/ "Programming Digest" rel="nofollow") (Weekly) — Broader engineering content with frequent .NET coverage.  
13. [Dev Leader Weekly](https://www.devleader.ca/newsletter/ "Dev Leader Weekly" rel="nofollow") (Weekly) — Leadership, culture, and architecture guidance for senior engineers.  
14. [DotNetLetter](https://dotnetletter.com/ "DotNetLetter" rel="nofollow") (Weekly) — Simple and clean .NET-centric content.  
15. [InboxReads – .NET Picks](https://www.inboxreads.co/ "InboxReads" rel="nofollow") (Curated) — A directory of the most community-recommended .NET newsletters.  
16. [DevBlogs – .NET News](https://devblogs.microsoft.com/dotnet/ "DevBlogs .NET" rel="nofollow") (On-demand) — Aggregates official .NET blog updates.  
17. [Buffered Insights](https://buffered.io/ "Buffered Insights" rel="nofollow") (Irregular) — Thoughtful, community-curated technical blog summaries.

## Cross-Discipline & Tech Context

18. [Tech Talks Weekly](https://techtalksweekly.substack.com/ "Tech Talks Weekly" rel="nofollow") — Watch recently uploaded conference talks (many include .NET topics).  
19. [Architecture Weekly](https://www.architecture-weekly.com/ "Architecture Weekly" rel="nofollow") — Curated software architecture resources, relevant to scalable .NET architectures.  
20. [The Pragmatic Engineer](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/ "The Pragmatic Engineer" rel="nofollow") — Wide ranging engineering leadership, culture, and growth commentary.  
21. [Big Tech Digest](https://bigtechdigest.substack.com/ "Big Tech Digest" rel="nofollow") — Aggregated articles from top tech companies; you'll often find .NET-themed case studies.  
22. [Changelog News](https://changelog.com/news "Changelog News" rel="nofollow") — Developer-focused, open-source news with occasional .NET project spotlights.

## Technical Leadership & Trends

23. [High Growth Engineer](https://www.highgrowthengineer.com/ "High Growth Engineer" rel="nofollow") (Jordan Cutler) — Career and engineering growth advice that resonates across tech stacks.  
24. [Developing Dev](https://developingdev.substack.com/ "Developing Dev" rel="nofollow") (Ryan Peterman) — Mentor-style advice tailored for early and mid-level developers.  
25. [Coding Challenges](https://codingchallenges.substack.com/ "Coding Challenges" rel="nofollow") (John Crickett) — Project-based learning via build-along newsletters.  
26. [Software Lead Weekly](https://softwareleadweekly.com/ "Software Lead Weekly" rel="nofollow") — Management and leadership insights—structured for technical leaders.

## Bonus Picks Worth Exploring

28. [Morning Brew – Tech](https://www.morningbrew.com/tech "Morning Brew Tech" rel="nofollow") — Daily tech summaries with a sharp tone—some .NET content shows up.

### How to Choose Wisely

- Pick 3–4 newsletters aligning with your current goals.  
- Block 10–15 minutes weekly to skim and save what matters.  
- Share appetizers from each at team lunch or standups.  
- Refresh your stack quarterly—drop what doesn’t stick, try a new one.

### Final Thoughts
Great engineers don’t just write code—they curate what they learn, constantly. These 30 free newsletters are handpicked to keep you energized, informed, and ahead of the curve in .NET and software leadership.
