>**Public Cloud Application Software or Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) is cloud-based, on-demand software offered to businesses or individuals that solve problems for them.**

For example, Salesforce provides CRM, Office 365 provides productivity tools, and Box provides File sharing are examples of SaaS software.

Many SaaS software offers **free subscriptions** based on limited usage, which is advantageous to startups, small business and entrepreneurs as they couldn't have afforded such a useful software otherwise.

Cost-effective, flexible subscription, pay only for what you use and no waste of time are some of the many advantages why so many businesses use SaaS software that are popular in their industry.

SaaS software market is continuously growing every year. There are still many opportunities exists to solve such industry specific problems.

SaaS software are changing the way businesses operate by solving their critical problems and help them save lots of time and cost.
